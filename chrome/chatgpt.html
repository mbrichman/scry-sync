ChatGPT → Scry Beta

Push your ChatGPT conversations into Scry, verbatim, with their image bytes. Runs entirely in your browser against chatgpt.com. Reads are read-only; nothing is ever deleted at the source.
You must be signed in to chatgpt.com in this browser. The first load fetches your conversation list; each push fetches that conversation's full history.
Pushing sends each conversation to Scry verbatim; Scry decides the visible branch and archives the full tree. With the image toggle below on, the original image bytes travel alongside so Scry can show them.
